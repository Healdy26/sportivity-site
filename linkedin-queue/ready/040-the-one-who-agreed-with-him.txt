The most dangerous person I ever worked alongside was not the one who argued with the boss.

It was the one who agreed with him.

Same language. Same certainty. Same confidence, only without any of the curiosity. Brought in not because he challenged the leadership but because he admired it.

At the time I read that as alignment. Loyalty. Everybody pulling the same way.

I see it differently now. He was not a colleague. He was an echo.

And here is what I have worked out since. Ego led leadership does not just do damage on its own. It copies itself. People watch what gets rewarded, and if what gets rewarded is agreement, then agreement is what you will be handed. Every time. By everyone. For years.

You end up with a room full of people who have learned that being close to you matters more than being straight with you.

Nobody sets out to build that. It builds itself while you are busy being pleased with how smoothly the meetings go.

So here is a hard one. On your own, no need to say it out loud.

Think of the last three people you brought in or promoted.

How many of them have ever told you that you were wrong?

If the answer is none, that is not a hiring problem. That is a culture telling you exactly what it rewards.

Your job was never to be agreed with. It is to be told the truth early enough to still do something about it.
