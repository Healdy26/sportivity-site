A lad told me recently he is about eighteen months off going out on his own.

He is good. Better than the person two rungs above him, and he knows it. That is most of the reason he wants out. He had the name, a website, a day rate worked out.

I asked him who chases the invoice when it does not get paid.

He laughed. Then he stopped laughing, because he had not thought about it once.

When you work for somebody else you do one job. You do the work, and you get judged on the work. Somebody above you quietly absorbs all the rest.

Go on your own and four jobs turn up. The one who does the work. The one who sells it. The one who collects the money. And the one that breaks people, the one who manages other humans doing the work you used to do yourself.

You have only ever been judged on the first one.

So learn the other three while you are still on their payroll. Price a job wrong where you are now and your boss has a slightly worse quarter. Price it wrong in year one of your own thing and you cannot pay your mortgage. Identical lesson. Wildly different invoice.

Find one thing in that building with money or people on the end of it, and ask to own it from start to finish. Not help with. Own.

If the whole lot landed on you on Monday morning, which of the four catches you out first?
