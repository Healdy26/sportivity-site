Most of the people who tell me AI is rubbish tried it once.

They asked it something, got a page of waffle back, and decided that was the ceiling.

They are not being awkward. Nobody ever told them the first answer is always the worst one.

I watched a director go through this with his whole company. Licences for everybody, launch email, lunchtime webinar. Six months later two people were using it properly and the rest had opened it once. He was fairly sure by then that the whole thing had been oversold.

It had not been oversold. His team had all had the same bad first go, on their own, with nobody around to tell them that bit is normal.

Here is what actually happens when it works.

You ask. What comes back is average. You tell it what is wrong with it, you give it the detail only you know, and you ask again. The third answer is the one worth having.

Nobody learns that in a webinar. They learn it from watching somebody else do it and not pretend it went smoothly.

So try this at your next team meeting. It costs you nothing.

Do not ask who has had a win with it. You will get silence, or you will get showing off.

Ask what people have tried that did not work.

That one question does more than a training day, because it turns a private embarrassment into a shared problem. And somebody sat in that room will have cracked the exact thing another person gave up on.

Who in your business reckons it is rubbish?

And has anyone ever sat with them while they had a second go?
