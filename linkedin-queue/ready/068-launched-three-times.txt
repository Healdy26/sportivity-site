Five years somewhere and you will have watched the same idea get launched three times.

New name on it. New deck. Same idea.

And by the second time round you did not put much in. Not because you are cynical. Because you had learned.

That is what churn does to a team, and most leaders never once count the cost of it.

Think about a department on its fourth head in six years. No plan survives that. Nothing ever gets seen through, because whoever started it has gone before it landed, and the next one arrives needing something of their own to be known for.

So the work never stops. The finishing stops.

And the people doing that work get very good at one particular skill. Waiting. They nod in the room, they go back to their desk, and they carry on exactly as they were, because so far they have been right every single time.

You cannot fix that with another launch. A fifth announcement does not undo four abandonments.

What does work is finishing one thing. One. Smaller than you want it to be.

Pick the thing that is already half done and nobody believes in any more. Finish it properly. Say out loud that it is finished, and name the people who got it over the line.

Then go and do the next one.

Holding steady is not glamorous and it will never trend. It is still the ground everything else gets built on, and you cannot buy it. You earn it by seeing one thing through while people watch.

So what got launched where you work that nobody ever finished, and nobody ever buried either?
