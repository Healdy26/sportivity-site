I lost a piece of work last year that I had genuinely expected to get.

Not hoped. Expected. I had built the whole thing in my head already, down to how I would run the first session.

I was fine for about an hour. Very adult about it.

Then I was not fine.

And here is what I have only worked out in the last couple of years. The email did not do the damage. The next two days did.

You never sit down and decide you are not good enough. Nobody does that on purpose. What happens is smaller. You go back over it, you find the answer you fumbled, and you build a quiet little line out of it. I am not quite at that level yet.

Then you carry on as normal and nothing looks different from the outside. But the line is in now, and it starts choosing for you. You put yourself up for one fewer thing. You do not ring the person you were going to ring.

So I have a rule. I am not allowed to decide anything about myself for forty eight hours.

Feel it. Be flat. Go and sulk by the water. But no verdicts for two days.

Then on the third day, ring them and ask one question. What would I have needed to be the obvious choice?

The answer is almost never the thing you spent two days chewing on.

What did you decide about yourself the last time something did not go your way?
