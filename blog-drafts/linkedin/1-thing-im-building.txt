You know when you've had a bad haircut and nobody says a word?

Everyone can see it. Your team can see it. And not one of them mentions it, because it's awkward and they've got to sit next to you all day.

That's leadership.

The higher you go, the more polite everyone gets. And polite is not the same as honest.

I've sat with good leaders, running decent businesses, genuinely trying, whose teams had quietly decided which things weren't worth raising with them. Nobody's being difficult. Everyone's being sensible. The boss is just the last to know.

So here's the small thing you can do tomorrow.

Stop asking "how am I doing?" You'll get a compliment, and compliments teach you nothing.

Ask "what's the one bit of how I run these meetings that annoys you?"

Then shut up. Count to five. The honest answer always arrives after the polite one, and only if you leave a gap big enough for it to climb through.
