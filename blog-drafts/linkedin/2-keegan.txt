Kevin Keegan lost a title live on television.

You know the clip. Finger jabbing, face going, the words falling out of him faster than he can catch them.

We've all watched it and had a laugh. I've watched it and felt something else.

Because that wasn't a man losing his temper. That was a man who cared so much he couldn't hide it, with a camera pointed at him at the worst possible moment.

His players ran through walls for him. That isn't tactics. You cannot fake it, and no leadership course sells it.

But here's the bit we skip past. The same open heart that made them run through walls is what left him standing there with nothing to hide behind. Newcastle didn't win the league.

Heart is a superpower and a risk in the same breath.

So lead with it anyway. Care out loud, in front of people, where they can see it. The alternative is being the leader nobody can read, and nobody runs through a wall for a locked door.

Just don't let the moment run the show. Feeling it and acting on it are two different things, and about four seconds apart.

Rest easy, Kevin.
