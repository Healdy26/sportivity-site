# The Monthly Edge: reusable how-to issue template

The shape every "how-to" issue follows, so we can flip them fast. Modelled on what works (clear hook, a map, step-by-step with screenshots, one clear ask). Andy's voice throughout: plain Northern English, no em dashes, one thought per line, no corporate filler.

How we use it: Andy names the topic ("this issue: how to use Claude to plan a week of content"). Claude fills the template with the full draft and marks exactly where screenshots go. Andy drops the screenshots in on Substack, checks it, hits send. Target: ~30 minutes an issue, not a day.

---

## Subject line
Curiosity or a clear payoff, under ~55 characters. Plus 2 alternatives.

## Preview text
One short line that earns the open.

## The hook (2-4 short lines)
An everyday metaphor or a bold, true claim that makes them stop. Then drop into the point. No preamble.

## The promise (1-2 lines)
Tell them exactly what they'll have done by the end. "By the time you finish this, you'll have [concrete result]."

## Why it matters now (short)
One tight paragraph. The real reason this is worth 10 minutes today.

## The map
The whole thing at a glance, so a beginner doesn't freeze.
> The 5 steps:
> 1. ...
> 2. ...
> 3. ...
> 4. ...
> 5. ...
Then: name which steps are mechanics and which are the real skill.

## The steps (one block each)
For every step:
- **What to do** in plain words.
- **The exact prompt or action** (in a code block they can copy).
- **What success looks like** so they know it worked.
- `[SCREENSHOT: describe what to capture]` so Andy knows exactly what to grab.

One step at a time. Don't move on until the last one works.

## The pro bit (the "mess")
The part Claude can't fully give them: the judgement, the thing that breaks, the tip from real experience. This is what keeps them subscribed.

## The ask (CTA to a paid product)
Point to the relevant paid thing in Andy's voice, not a hard sell.
- Coaching/quality topic → CQI: https://www.sportivityconsultants.com/cqi
- Leadership/consultancy topic → book a chat: https://calendly.com/andy-sportivityconsultants/30min
- Later: the LMS subscription.

## Share + subscribe
One clear share ask ("send this to one person who needs it"), then the subscribe line for anyone reading a forward.

## Sign-off
Short, warm, Andy. The grounding close fits the heavier issues: "feel your feet on the floor. You are here. You are here."
