## One honest word on the pace

Six catch-up emails in a fortnight, on top of a newsletter, is a lot of Andy in somebody's inbox. It is the right call because these posts are good and nobody has seen them, but watch the unsubscribes after the first two.

If it starts biting, Substack lets you publish to the web without emailing. Switch the remaining catch-up ones to web-only and the archive still fills, people just find them rather than get them. The ones from 31 October onwards are current, so those should always email.
