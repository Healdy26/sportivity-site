Everyone has seen the eight grand headline. Hardly anybody has worked out what they would actually get.

It is not a cheque. It is a stack, and most of it comes with conditions.

The biggest single lump is the Youth Jobs Grant. Three thousand pounds for taking on an 18 to 24 year old who has been on Universal Credit and looking for work for six months or more. It is paid in two goes, eighteen hundred at month two and twelve hundred at month five.

Then, if you go down the apprenticeship route, there is two thousand for a smaller employer taking on a 16 to 24 year old, two thousand for the foundation apprenticeship incentive, and a thousand for the younger ones and for care leavers. No employer National Insurance on an under 21 either.

But the change most people have missed is the quiet big one. Fully funded apprenticeship training for non-levy employers used to stop at 21. It now covers all eligible 16 to 24 year olds.

So if you looked at an apprentice last year and the training cost put you off, that objection has gone.

Have you worked out your actual number yet?
