## 2026-08-05 — Leadership apprenticeships defunded from 1 Sept: Team Leader L3, Ops Manager L5, Chartered Manager L6, Coaching Professional L5

**Item:** 16 apprenticeship standards lose funding for **new starts from 1 September 2026**. Four of them are the country's main leadership and coaching routes: **Team Leader / Supervisor (L3)**, **Operations / Departmental Manager (L5)**, **Chartered Manager Degree Apprenticeship (L6)** and **Coaching Professional (L5)**. Team Leader is the most popular apprenticeship in Britain to lose funding: **12,670 starts in 2024-25** across ~450 training providers, of which only **80 were under 19**. Anyone who **starts before 1 September** finishes on existing funding, so the window is open for another few weeks. **No replacement standard has been announced for most of them.** Government rationale is redirecting money to young people and technical skills, citing a ~40% decline in young apprenticeship starts over a decade. Ben Rowland, CEO of AELP, said the government is dismantling employer-led programmes employers actually cherished. Funded alternatives being pointed at: Associate Project Manager (L4) and AI & Automation Practitioner (L4). Direct-funded CMI qualifications (L3 to L7) stay available outside the apprenticeship system.

**Timeliness — be straight about this:** the defunding list was announced **18 March 2026**, so it is not a fresh announcement. What makes it live *this week* is the deadline: the new funding year started **1 August** (rules v3 published 29 July), and the enrolment cliff is **1 September, 27 days away**. Providers and employers are scrambling now. Judged as a closing-window story, not a breaking one. Andy's call on whether that's timely enough.

**Why it clears the bar:** This is the strongest connective take Andy has had in weeks, because it collides head-on with the item banked on 3 August. From this month there is up to **£8,000** of public money to hire a 16 to 24 year old. From next month there is **nothing** to train the person who has to lead them. Nobody in the FE press is joining those two dots — they're covering it as a sector funding story. Andy can cover it as a leadership story, which is his whole business. Extra authority: **Coaching Professional L5 is Andy's own professional standard**, and Team Leader / Ops Manager are exactly the routes his Workforce Development & Qualifications and Leadership Development service lines sit on top of. He also sees the UA92 end of the pipeline.

**Persona/target:** JOHN primarily (owner/director with a first-line manager who was promoted for being good at the job, no training behind them). Strong secondary for DAN — Team Leader L3 was *his* route up, and it just closed. Also lands with Andy's education/FE network.

## 2026-08-03 — Youth employment package: up to £8k for SMEs to hire a young person (BUILT INTO DRAFTS, not a same-day react)

**Status:** Logged, not banked as a LinkedIn react. The announcement was 28 July, so the same-day reactive window had already passed by the time the radar caught it. Andy's call: build it into long-form instead. Done, see below.

**Item:** Burnham government's youth employment / NEETs package. From **August 2026**, apprenticeship training is fully funded for non-levy employers (pay bill under £3m) for **all 16 to 24 year olds** (previously capped at 21 unless EHCP or care leaver). The **Youth Jobs Grant** pays **£3,000** for hiring an 18 to 24 year old on Universal Credit and jobseeking 6+ months, live since 30 June, paid £1,800 at month 2 and £1,200 at month 5, initially 18 to 21 and widening to 18 to 24 in autumn. From **October 2026**, a **£2,000** non-levy hiring grant for 16 to 24 year olds, plus a **£2,000** foundation apprenticeship incentive and **£1,000** young apprentice / care leaver payment. Total employer support stacks to roughly **£8,000**. No employer NI on under 21s, or apprentices under 25 earning below £50,270. A **£4,500** household bursary for Universal Credit families is being designed (amounts TBC) to fix the benefits cliff-edge, one cited case had a single parent with a disabled child losing ~£340/week against apprentice earnings of £258. Technical routes for **14 year olds** from Year 10 land September 2028. Context: **over 1 million NEETs, ~1 in 8** young people in England; £287m for 22,000+ college places; target of 50,000 new youth apprenticeships this parliament.

**Why it's worth long-form even though it missed the react window:** Dead centre of Andy's Workforce Development & Qualifications service line and his UA92 side. He sees both ends of the gap, the young people who want a first go and the owners who cannot see how to make it work. The differentiated take nobody else is giving: the money was never the blocker. Capability was. It pairs perfectly with the CMI numbers already used in the September newsletter (6% of managers think young recruits are ready; 82% are accidental managers), which turns "here's some free money" into "a subsidy makes it cheaper to repeat the thing that failed last time".

## 2026-07-24 — PE and Sport Premium scrapped, replaced by a national Partnerships Network

**Item:** The £320m/year PE and Sport Premium (direct to primary schools since the London 2012 legacy) is being scrapped and replaced by a national **PE and School Sport Partnerships Network**, launching spring 2027. Money will no longer land directly in schools — it flows through a national delivery partner working with the NGBs. £1bn over three years total (£580m network + ~£200m facilities + transition), but roughly 22% less per year than the old premium. **Timely peg:** on 10 July 2026 the DfE rebalanced transition funding up from £100m to £150m and extended the transition period, right after National School Sport Week — so the sector is actively debating this now and the network's design decisions are live.

**Why it clears the bar:** This is dead centre of Andy's CQI world — coaching quality in PE and school sport, mapped to CIMSPA standards and Ofsted, built across 2,000+ coaches. The announcement promises "quality-assured coaches" but names no framework for *how* quality is assured. That gap is exactly what CQI answers, and Andy can speak to it from authority. Ecosystem in play: DfE, Sport England, Active Partnerships, NGBs, afPE, Youth Sport Trust, CIMSPA.

**Persona/target:** Andy's own sector (sport / physical-activity + education) and his professional network — not Dan or John. Authority-marketing play tied directly to CQI.

**Sources:**
- Schools Week — https://schoolsweek.co.uk/dfe-scraps-pe-and-sport-premium-in-favour-of-partnerships-network/
- Public Sector Executive — https://www.publicsectorexecutive.com/articles/government-launches-pe-and-school-sport-partnerships-network-tackle-youth-inactivity
- Landscape & Amenity (10 Jul funding update) — https://landscapeandamenity.com/sections/play-and-activity/articles/2026-07-10/government-delivers-pe-and-school-sport-funding-update

**LinkedIn draft:**
